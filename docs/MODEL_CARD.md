# Model Card — Educational Baseline v1

## Intended use
Portfolio/research demonstration of an explainable wearable-feature pipeline.

## Inputs
Age, resting heart rate, HRV, sleep duration and steps/activity.

## Output
A bounded educational risk score/band with explanation.

## Data
Synthetic examples only in this repository.

## Limitations
No clinical validation, calibration or diagnostic performance is established. Wearable measurements vary by device and context. Important clinical risk factors are omitted.

## Prohibited interpretation
Do not treat output as diagnosis, medical advice, emergency triage or a validated event probability.
